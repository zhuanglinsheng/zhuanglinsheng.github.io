## Biography

Currently, I am a postdoctoral researcher at Center for Intelligent Computing,
Greater Bay University, focusing on intelligent constraint programming (CP)
algorithms and solver development.

I earned my Ph.D. in Operations Research from the National University of
Singapore in 2025. Earlier, I received a Master’s (2020) and a Bachelor’s (2017)
degree in Economics from Peking University.

## Research Interests

My research interest is broad, including stochastic modeling, optimization theory and algorithms.
